## Supported reusable route
1. Open https://rangareddy.dcourts.gov.in/ and select Case Status, Case Number, or open its observed official link https://rangareddy.dcourts.gov.in/case-status-search-by-case-number/.
2. Choose Court Complex or Court Establishment, then the known court. Wait for Case Type options to load.
3. Select case type, enter number and year, read and solve the displayed CAPTCHA under existing user authorization, and press Search. If the image is broken, use its ordinary Refresh Image link and wait for loading. No hidden endpoint requests are necessary.
4. Open View Case Details. An additional CAPTCHA may be displayed; complete it if asked. It appeared for FCOP735/2019 but not MC121/2023 in this session.
5. Match case type/number/year, filing details, CNR, court and parties before attributing identity. Preserve masked names and office-derived associations rather than claiming an independent name match.
6. Where Business On Date is a real link, open the latest date to read daily business. Some records expose only non-clickable date cells. Do not guess private endpoints or conclude there are no orders, costs or arrears.
7. Record actual checked time, query, source, result and unverified fields. Resume the rotating per-entry queue using this route for supported Ranga Reddy establishments instead of marking all district checks blocked.
